# Medical safety

Rohith Health Coach is a personal information and habit-reflection tool, not a medical device, clinician, diagnosis service, emergency monitor, or medication manager.

- Wearables may be missing, delayed, misclassified, or inaccurate.
- The system does not diagnose conditions or determine why a metric changed.
- It does not recommend starting, stopping, or changing a medicine or treatment.
- It does not replace professional care, laboratory testing, or emergency services.
- Urgent self-reported symptoms bypass ordinary coaching and display fixed guidance to contact local emergency services or an appropriate clinician. No automated risk score is generated.

Every narrative must preserve source limitations, data freshness, completeness, and baseline maturity. Model-specific use remains disabled until its fixed safety evaluation passes and the owner explicitly consents.
