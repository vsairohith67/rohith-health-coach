# AI consent

AI explanation is optional and separate from deterministic health processing. Consent must be off by default, specific to the provider, freely revocable, and recorded without health values.

The UI must explain: purpose (plain-language explanation only), exact aggregate categories shared, what is excluded, local vs external processing, provider identity, retention/training uncertainty or confirmed policy, cost, limitations, and that deterministic results remain available without consent.

Withdrawal disables new calls immediately and revokes provider/MCP tokens where applicable. It does not falsely promise deletion from provider backups; the applicable policy must be stated. Notes, GPS, medication, raw FIT, and raw samples require separate future review and are prohibited in RC4 regardless of generic consent.
